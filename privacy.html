Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Location data

If you allow it, this site uses your browser's geolocation to find your current coordinates and send them to our server, which queries Google's Places API for nearby restaurants. Your coordinates are not stored on our servers or shared with anyone other than Google, as part of that lookup. You can decline location access and enter a zip code instead; the zip code is converted to coordinates using Google's Geocoding service in the same way.

Cookies and ads

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you are in the EEA or UK, you will see a consent prompt before any personalized ads or non-essential cookies are set.

Third-party services

This site relies on the Google Maps Platform (Maps JavaScript API, Places API, Geocoding API) to display the map and search results, and on Google AdSense to show ads. Your use of this site is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

We don't require an account, don't store your search history, and don't sell your data.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13.
